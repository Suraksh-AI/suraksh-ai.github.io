Encrypted Face Matching and Search

This demo showcases our encrypted face template matching technology. It performs both 1:1 face verification and 1:N face search. A probe face embedding is matched against a gallery of embeddings, and both the probe and the gallery templates are encrypted. The matching process itself is fully encrypted, ensuring the data remains private.
